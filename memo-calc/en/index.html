Privacy Policy

This page explains how "Memo Calculator: Keep Results" (the "App", iPhone, iPad and Android versions) handles information about its users.

1. Operator

Studio Beita

2. Information the App collects

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. There is no account registration.

Your saved memos (names and numbers) and settings (such as theme, language and tax rate) are stored only on your device and are never sent to anyone, including the operator. Deleting the App deletes this data as well. (A "Remove ads" purchase can be brought back with "Restore purchase" in Settings.)

3. In-app purchase

The App offers "Remove ads", a one-time purchase that removes the ad at the bottom of the screen. Payment is handled by Apple (App Store) on iPhone and iPad, and by Google (Google Play) on Android. The operator never receives payment information such as your credit card number.

To check whether you have made the purchase, the App receives purchase records (such as the product, purchase date and transaction ID) from the store (App Store or Google Play) and uses them only on your device. These records are never sent to the operator.

4. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (iPhone and iPad: IDFA, only if you allow tracking / Android: advertising ID)
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Information about ad views and interactions, and about your device type and operating system
  • Diagnostic information about crashes and performance

iPhone and iPad: You can choose whether to allow tracking on the prompt shown the first time you open the App (after you close the guide), and change it at any time in the Settings app on your iPhone or iPad under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads). Once deleted, ads will no longer be personalized for you.

If you do not allow tracking or delete the advertising ID, the ads simply become less relevant to you; every feature of the App still works.

In the United Kingdom and some other regions, a consent screen may be shown as required by law. You can change your choice later in the App under Settings → Ads → "Privacy settings (ad consent)".

Once you purchase "Remove ads", ads are no longer shown.

For how Google handles information, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

5. Sharing with third parties

The operator does not provide your information to third parties (other than the information collected directly by the store and the advertising service above).

6. Children

The App is not directed at children. Users under 13 should use it with the consent of a parent or guardian, and should ask a parent or guardian before making an in-app purchase.

7. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be announced on this page.
