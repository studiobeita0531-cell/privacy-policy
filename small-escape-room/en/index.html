Privacy Policy

This page explains how "Escape from the Little Study" (the "App", iPhone and Android versions) handles information about its users.

1. Operator

Studio Beita

2. Information the App collects

The App does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or phone number. There is no account registration.

Your game progress and settings (such as volume and language) are stored only on your device and are never sent to anyone, including the operator. Deleting the App deletes this data as well.

3. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising identifier (iPhone: IDFA, only if you allow tracking / Android: advertising ID)
  • Approximate location estimated from your IP address
  • Information about ad views and interactions, and about your device type and operating system
  • Diagnostic information about crashes and performance

iPhone: You can choose whether to allow tracking on the prompt shown at first launch, and change it at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android: You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (on some devices, Settings → Privacy → Ads). Once deleted, ads will no longer be personalized for you.

If you do not allow tracking or delete the advertising ID, the ads simply become less relevant to you; you can still play the whole game.

In the United Kingdom and some other regions, a consent screen may be shown as required by law.

For how Google handles information, please see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

4. Sharing with third parties

The operator does not provide your information to third parties (other than the information collected directly by the advertising service above).

5. Children

The App is not directed at children. Users under 13 should use it with the consent of a parent or guardian.

6. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be announced on this page.
